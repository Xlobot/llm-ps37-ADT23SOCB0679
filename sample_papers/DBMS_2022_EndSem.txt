Total Marks: 50 | Time: 2 Hours
Instructions: Answer all questions.

Q1. (a) Define DBMS. Compare File Processing System with Database Management System. [5 Marks]
Q1. (b) Explain the Three-Schema Architecture of DBMS with a neat diagram. [5 Marks]

Q2. (a) What is an Entity-Relationship (ER) model? Draw an ER diagram for a University Management System. [6 Marks]
Q2. (b) Explain Primary Key, Candidate Key, Foreign Key, and Super Key with examples. [4 Marks]

Q3. (a) What is Relational Algebra? Explain Selection, Projection, Union, and Cartesian Product operations. [6 Marks]
Q3. (b) Explain First Normal Form (1NF), Second Normal Form (2NF), and Third Normal Form (3NF) with sample relations. [6 Marks]

Q4. (a) Explain ACID properties of transactions with suitable real-world banking examples. [6 Marks]
Q4. (b) What is Concurrency Control? Explain Two-Phase Locking (2PL) protocol. [6 Marks]

Q5. (a) What is Indexing in databases? Differentiate between Primary Index, Secondary Index, and Clustering Index. [6 Marks]
